About

Incoming 3rd year ECE student. Researcher. Builder.

I'm an incoming 3rd year Electrical & Computer Engineering Dean's List student at the University of Washington Seattle with a 3.84 ECE GPA. I thrive at the intersection of hardware and software through writing firmware, designing PCBs, and running acoustic experiments.

At the Networking & Emerging Wireless Technologies Lab, I contribute to ultrasonic acoustic research and underwater acoustics. I've conducted field deployments for underwater acoustics that fed into an ACM SenSys 2026 accepted paper with a 2.2° average lake deployment error. I'm also a Hardware/Firmware Developer at the Fitness Tech Engineering Student Organization at UW, building ESP32-based embedded systems with custom PCBs.

Outside of engineering, I serve as a Resident Adviser supporting a 900+ student residential community, directly advising 60 residents.
