About

2nd year ECE student. Researcher. Builder.

I'm a 2nd year Electrical & Computer Engineering student at the University of Washington Seattle with a 3.84 GPA and a spot on the Dean's List. I work at the intersection of hardware and software — writing firmware, designing PCBs, and running acoustic experiments.

At the NEWT Lab, I contribute to ultrasonic acoustic research — including field deployments that fed into an ACM SenSys 2026 accepted paper with a 2.2° average lake deployment error. I'm also a Hardware/Firmware Developer at Fitness Tech at UW, building ESP32-based embedded systems with custom PCBs.

Outside of engineering, I serve as a Resident Adviser supporting a 900+ student residential community, and I love tackling problems where theory meets the real world.
