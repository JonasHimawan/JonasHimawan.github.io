Project Details

Embedded hardware for smart hydration tracking.

SipSync is a smart water bottle concept designed to track hydration behavior and connect with a mobile app. As part of the Fitness Tech hardware development team, I worked on embedded hardware and firmware development using ESP32-based systems, sensor integration, Bluetooth connectivity, and custom PCB design.

The project follows a complete hardware development pipeline: breadboard prototyping, circuit design, sensor testing, firmware development, PCB design, soldering, and validation. This page can be expanded with photos, schematics, PCB screenshots, and demo videos as the project develops further.
